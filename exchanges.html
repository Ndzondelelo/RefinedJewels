Wrong finish or style? Contact us promptly and we will check whether another available option can be arranged.

Exchange window

Eligible exchange requests should be made within 7 calendar days after delivery or collection. The item must be unused, unworn, undamaged and accompanied by its original packaging, tags and included accessories.

Availability

Exchanges depend on current stock. Refined Jewels cannot reserve a replacement until the exchange has been confirmed. If the preferred option is unavailable, the customer may choose another available item or discuss an eligible return.

Price differences

If the replacement costs more, the difference must be paid using the confirmed secure payment method before it is released. If it costs less, the difference will be handled using an agreed refund or credit method.

Costs

For preference-based exchanges, the customer pays the return and replacement-delivery costs. If Refined Jewels supplied the wrong product or confirms a defect, costs and remedies will be handled in accordance with applicable consumer law.

Items that cannot be exchanged

  • Pierced earrings after sealed packaging has been opened or the earrings have been tried on, unless faulty or incorrect.
  • Personalised, altered or specially sourced items, unless faulty or incorrect.
  • Items showing wear, accidental damage, misuse, missing parts or inadequate care.

These limitations do not remove rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

How to exchange

  1. Contact Refined Jewels on WhatsApp with the order details.
  2. State the preferred replacement finish, colour, size or style.
  3. Send clear photographs if requested.
  4. Wait for stock confirmation and return instructions before sending anything.
