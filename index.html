USEFUL TIPS

Keep your favourites
looking refined.

A little care helps jewellery retain its colour, shine, and finish.

01

Last on, first off

Put jewellery on after perfume and lotion, then remove it before bathing, swimming, exercising, or sleeping.

02

Store separately

Keep each piece in a soft pouch or dry compartment to reduce scratching, tangling, and contact with moisture.

03

Clean gently

After wearing, wipe pieces with a clean, soft cloth. Avoid harsh chemicals and abrasive cleaning products.
