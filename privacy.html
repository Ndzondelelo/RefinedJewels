YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy, in plain language.

This notice explains how Refined Jewels handles personal information when customers browse, enquire or place an order.

Information we may receive

  • Name and contact details supplied during an enquiry or order.
  • WhatsApp messages, selected products and order preferences.
  • Delivery or collection information, including an address when delivery is requested.
  • Payment confirmation or transaction references supplied by a payment provider. Refined Jewels does not ask customers to enter card details directly on this website.
  • Information voluntarily supplied for a return, exchange, review or customer-service request.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, confirm availability, fulfil and deliver orders, provide customer service, prevent fraud, maintain transaction records, and meet legal or accounting duties. Information will not be sold.

Basket storage and cookies

The website stores basket contents and checkout preferences in the customer’s browser using local storage. This lets the basket work between pages. Refined Jewels does not currently use advertising trackers or analytics cookies on this static website. Browser storage can be cleared through the browser settings.

Service providers

Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary with services involved in the request, such as WhatsApp, a courier, website hosting, and a selected hosted payment provider such as Yoco or PayFast once live payment links are enabled. Those providers process information under their own privacy terms.

Retention and security

Personal information is kept only for as long as reasonably needed for the order, customer service, dispute handling and applicable legal or record-keeping duties. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no internet or messaging service can be guaranteed completely secure. Customers should never send full card details, PINs or one-time passwords through WhatsApp.

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, customers may ask what personal information Refined Jewels holds about them and request correction or deletion of inaccurate or unnecessary information. They may also object to certain processing or withdraw consent where consent is the basis used.

Requests can be made through the WhatsApp contact below. Customers may also learn about privacy rights or submit an eligible complaint through the Information Regulator South Africa.

Children and updates

This website and catalogue are intended for customers able to make a lawful purchase. Refined Jewels does not knowingly seek personal information from children. This notice may be updated when the ordering process or service providers change; the latest version will remain on this page.

This notice is designed around the transparency principles in South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).
