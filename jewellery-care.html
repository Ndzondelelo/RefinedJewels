CARE FOR YOUR COLLECTION

Keep every piece beautiful.

Gentle handling, careful cleaning and dry storage help jewellery and watches keep their finish for longer.

Everyday care

  • Put jewellery on after perfume, lotion, hairspray and cosmetics have dried.
  • Remove pieces before bathing, swimming, exercising, cleaning or sleeping.
  • Avoid chlorine, salt water, household chemicals and prolonged moisture.
  • Handle clasps, chains, stones and watch crowns gently.
  • After wear, wipe the item with a clean, soft and dry cloth.

Gold-plated pieces

Gold plating is a surface finish that can gradually wear with friction, moisture, sweat and chemicals. Do not use abrasive polish, brushes or jewellery dips. Wipe gently with a soft dry cloth and store the piece separately to reduce scratching and rubbing.

Silver-tone and stainless-steel pieces

Use a soft cloth for routine cleaning. If the product description confirms stainless steel, a lightly damp soft cloth may be used for marks, followed immediately by thorough drying. Avoid abrasive cleaners and do not assume stones, plating or attached materials have the same water resistance as the metal.

Pearls and delicate stones

Pearls and mother-of-pearl can be affected by perfume, acids, heat and harsh cleaners. Wipe them gently after wear. Do not soak them, scrub them or use ultrasonic cleaning unless a qualified jeweller confirms it is safe for the complete piece.

Watches

Follow the water-resistance rating in the specific product description. A rating should not be treated as permission for hot showers, steam, diving or operating buttons under water unless the manufacturer expressly allows it. Keep leather straps dry and wipe metal straps gently after wear.

Storage

Store each item clean and dry, ideally in its own soft pouch or compartment, away from direct sunlight, humidity and extreme temperatures. Fasten chains before storage to help prevent tangling.

Normal wear

Fine scratches, gradual plating wear and changes caused by skin chemistry or exposure can occur over time and are not always manufacturing defects. Contact Refined Jewels if a new item appears faulty or if you are unsure how to care for a particular piece.
