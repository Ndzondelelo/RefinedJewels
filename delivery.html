CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Delivery & collection.

How your order moves from confirmation to collection or delivery, with costs agreed before you pay.

Where we deliver

Refined Jewels offers collection by arrangement, local delivery, and nationwide courier delivery within South Africa. Availability for a specific address is confirmed on WhatsApp before payment.

Options and estimates

  • Collection: the collection location and available time are agreed on WhatsApp.
  • Local delivery: normally estimated at 1–3 business days after payment and dispatch confirmation.
  • Nationwide courier: normally estimated at 3–7 business days after payment and dispatch confirmation.

These are estimates, not guaranteed delivery dates. Weekends, public holidays, remote areas, courier disruption and severe weather may add time.

Delivery costs

Delivery is not included in the product total shown in the basket. The cost depends on the destination and delivery method and is confirmed on WhatsApp before the customer pays.

Do not make a payment until Refined Jewels has confirmed product availability, the delivery fee and the secure payment method.

Dispatch and tracking

Orders are prepared after availability and cleared payment are confirmed. When tracking is available, the tracking details are shared with the customer. Customers should check that their name, mobile number and delivery address are correct before approving the order.

Receiving your order

Please inspect the parcel as soon as practical. If the parcel appears damaged, the wrong item arrives, or anything is missing, contact Refined Jewels on WhatsApp with the order details and clear photographs. Reporting visible transit damage within 48 hours helps us investigate quickly, but does not limit rights provided by applicable law.

Address changes and delays

Send address changes before dispatch. Once a courier has collected the parcel, rerouting may not be possible and extra courier charges may apply. Refined Jewels will assist with reasonable tracking enquiries but cannot control delays caused by the courier or circumstances outside our reasonable control.

Need delivery help?

Send your suburb or province and the items you want. We will confirm the available option and cost.
