Returns, made clear.

Please contact us before sending anything back so we can confirm eligibility and provide return instructions.

Change-of-mind returns

Refined Jewels accepts eligible change-of-mind return requests made within 7 calendar days after delivery or collection. The item must be unused, unworn, undamaged and returned with its original packaging, tags and included accessories.

For hygiene reasons, pierced earrings cannot be returned after their sealed packaging has been opened or after they have been tried on, unless they are defective or the incorrect item was supplied. Personalised or specially sourced items cannot be returned for change of mind.

Incorrect, damaged or defective items

If an item arrives damaged, is not what was ordered, or appears defective, contact us with the order details and photographs. Where the Consumer Protection Act applies, this policy does not remove or restrict the customer’s statutory rights, including the remedies available for goods that do not meet the required standards of quality.

Please report visible delivery damage within 48 hours where practical so the courier issue can be investigated promptly. A later report will still be assessed and this request does not limit rights under applicable law.

How to request a return

  1. Contact Refined Jewels on WhatsApp before sending the item.
  2. Provide the order details, reason for return and photographs where relevant.
  3. Wait for return instructions and the agreed return method.
  4. Pack the item securely with all original components.

Items sent without prior confirmation may be delayed because we may not be able to identify the order.

Return costs and inspection

For an eligible change-of-mind return, the customer pays the return cost and remains responsible for the parcel until it is received. If Refined Jewels confirms that the wrong item was supplied or the item was defective or damaged on delivery, reasonable return costs will be handled as required by applicable law.

Returned items are inspected before the return is approved. Wear, misuse, accidental damage, missing parts or damage caused by not following the care guidance may affect eligibility, subject always to rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Refunds

Approved refunds are arranged using an agreed secure method, normally within 10 business days after inspection. Bank or payment-provider processing times may add time before the funds appear. Original delivery fees are refunded only where required by law or agreed because Refined Jewels supplied an incorrect or verified defective item.

Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude rights that customers have under South African consumer law.
